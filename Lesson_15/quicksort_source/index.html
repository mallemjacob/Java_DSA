04 / UNDER THE HOOD

The logic

quicksort.pseudo
quickSort(A, low, high):
pivot = A[high]
i = low - 1
for j = low to high - 1:
if A[j] ≤ pivot:
i++; swap(A[i], A[j])
swap(A[i + 1], A[high])
quickSort(left)
quickSort(right)
